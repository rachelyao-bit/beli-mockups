📊 How badges work
You earn two separate badges per format type — one global, one for your home city (NYC). City and global pools can have different thresholds.

⚡ NYC Bars is harder than global (15 vs. 13 needed) — NYC's bar scene is unusually competitive.

Badges are permanent once earned — no expiry, no refresh window.
